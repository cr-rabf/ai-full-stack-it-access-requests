## Open Questions

| Decision | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Supported shared-operation arrangement** — R-07 | Whether operators share one host/storage or use separate installations materially affects storage and concurrency design. |
| **Which attempt becomes current** — R-09, R-11–R-12 | Overlapping attempts and source updates need a defined precedence rule so an older completion cannot silently replace a newer result. |
| **Attempt, completion and recovery boundaries** — R-08, R-12, R-50 | Persistence and retry behavior depend on what counts as completed and what must survive interruption. |
| **Reuse and refresh rules** — R-13, R-48 | The plan needs to identify which settings invalidate results and how explicit retries interact with cached failures. |
| **Accuracy comparison rules** — R-57–R-58 | Expected fields, normalization and denominator rules should be fixed before measuring outputs to avoid adjusting the evaluation to fit results. |
